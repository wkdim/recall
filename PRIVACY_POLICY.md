# Privacy Policy for Recall

_Last updated: 2026-07-30_

Recall is an offline-first flashcard app. This policy explains what
information the app handles and, just as importantly, what it doesn't.

## No account required

Recall doesn't require you to sign up, log in, or provide any personal
information to use it.

## Your flashcards stay on your device

All decks, cards, review history, streaks, and settings are stored locally
on your device, in a local database. None of it is uploaded anywhere,
because there is no server for it to go to — Recall has no backend.

## On-device AI features

The "Generate from text" and "Improve with AI" features run a small
open-source language model entirely on your device. The first time you use
either feature, the app downloads that model file from Hugging Face
(huggingface.co) — a one-time download, after which the feature works fully
offline. That download request, like any file download, is visible to the
hosting provider in the ordinary way any request is (e.g. your IP address).
No flashcard content, no text you type, and no results from these features
are ever sent off your device — all of the actual AI processing happens
locally.

## Crash reporting

Recall uses [Sentry](https://sentry.io) to report crashes and errors, so
problems can be found and fixed. If the app crashes or hits an unexpected
error, Sentry receives a crash report containing: the error/stack trace,
device model and OS version, and the app's version number. It does **not**
receive your flashcard content, anything you've typed, or any other data
from within the app — crash reports are limited to the technical details
needed to diagnose the crash itself.

## No ads, no tracking, no data sale

Recall has no advertising, no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs, and
does not sell or share data with anyone — because it doesn't collect data
to begin with.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes — for example, if a crash-reporting service is
added — this document will be updated and the "Last updated" date above
will reflect it.
